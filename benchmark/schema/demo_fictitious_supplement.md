## Figure S1. Chart open-to-close time by calendar month

Line plot. The horizontal axis is calendar month from April 2023 to March 2024. The vertical
axis is mean chart open-to-close time in minutes, labelled *minutes (linear scale)*.

The vertical axis carries tick marks at the following values, drawn at equal pixel spacing:

| Tick label | Distance from origin (mm) |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 |
| 10 | 18 |
| 20 | 36 |
| 40 | 54 |
| 80 | 72 |

Four series are plotted, one per cluster, each showing a step down in the month of
activation. Cluster 1 falls from 21.9 to 14.2 minutes, cluster 2 from 21.4 to 13.8, cluster 3
from 22.0 to 14.1, and cluster 4 from 21.1 to 13.5.

## Figure S3. Subgroup analysis by postgraduate year

Forest plot of the adjusted mean difference in chart open-to-close time, in minutes, with the
null at zero and negative values favouring the intervention.

| Subgroup | Encounters | Adjusted mean difference (95% CI) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PGY 1–2 | 9,842 | −11.2 (−14.0 to −8.4) |
| PGY 3–20 | 21,363 | −5.1 (−7.3 to −2.9) |
| Overall | 31,205 | −8.4 (−7.9 to −3.2) |

Test for interaction: p = 0.01.

## Note on ascertainment of return visits

Return visits were identified by matching the patient identifier within the index hospital's
record system. Because the participating hospitals did not share a common patient identifier
during the study period, a patient attending a different hospital in the group after an index
visit would not have been counted as a return.
